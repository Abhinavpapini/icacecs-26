ICACECS 2026


The Department of Computer Science and Engineering, VNRVJIET, successfully organized a 2-Day International Conference "International Conference on Advances in Computer Engineering and Communication Systems (ICACECS)" (ICACECS 2020, ICACECS 2021, ICACECS 2022, ICACECS 2023, ICACECS 2024 and ICACECS 2025) consecutively for 6 years. This conference is conducted in association with "Springer Nature International" the publication partner and technically sponsored by Computer Society of India (CSI). Every year, there is an overwhelming response from researchers around the globe publishing their research contributions. Continuing this legacy, the 7th International Conference ICACECS 2026 is now scheduled to be conducted on 6th and 7th August 2026 in online with the themes "Smart Innovations in Mezzanine Technologies, Data Analytics, Knowledge Engineering Networks, and Communication Systems" and "Intelligent Systems through Human-Centric Technologies and Data Driven Insights" which is the theme of the conference. The 7th International Conference ICACECS 2026 is now scheduled to be conducted on 6th and 7th August 2026 in collaboration with University of North Texas, College of Information, USA, co-located at Presidency University, Bengaluru, India.

ICACECS 2025 is organized in collaboration with Vignana Jyothi Institute of Management, Hyderabad, India, Institut Mines-Telecom Business School, Evry-Courcouronnes, France, The Assam Kaziranga University, Assam, India and Mohan Babu University, Tirupati, India.
ICACECS 2024 is organized in collaboration with THE UNIVERSITY OF THE WEST INDIES (UWI), AT MONA, JAMAICA and Mohan Babu University, Tirupati, India.
ICACECS 2023 is organized in collaboration with THE UNIVERSITY OF THE WEST INDIES (UWI), AT MONA, JAMAICA.

Authors are solicited to contribute their submissions illustrating research results, innovations, and significant advances in the fields of Artificial Intelligence, Machine learning, Smart Systems, Networks, and Communication Systems, Quantum computing, Knowledge Engineering and Ontology, Internet of Things, Education Technology and Business Engineering.

ICACECS 2026 is a Convergence forum bringing together scholars from different countries to participate and transform the Research Landscape of the globe and carve a Road Map for Implementation. It provides a valuable networking opportunity and brings a new era for the Research scholars, Students, Professors, and Industrialists providing insights to the recent trends and developments in the field of Computer Science with a special focus on Mezzanine technologies.

Call For Papers

7th INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON ADVANCES IN COMPUTER ENGINEERING AND COMMUNICATION SYSTEMS (ICACECS-2026) invites original work in the broad areas of Computer Engineering and Communication Systems. The major themes are identified (but not limited to) and listed below under the Conference Tracks.

AUTHOR GUIDELINES

Important Notice: The submission of research papers is presently restricted to foreign authors, in accordance with the requests received via email from the majority of our international contributors.
  • Authors should submit their papers only through the Microsoft CMT Conference link. Create/sign up for a Microsoft CMT account and upload your paper.
  • The papers which are not in the prescribed format will be subjected to rejection without any judgment on its quality.
  • All the final camera ready papers must be submitted in the latex format only
  • Plagiarism report of the content exceeding the acceptable range will not be permitted for presentation.
  • All papers that conform to submission guidelines will be peer-reviewed and evaluated based on originality, innovation, technical and/or research content/depth, correctness, relevance to conference, contributions, and readability.
  • At least one author of an accepted paper should register for their paper to be presented/published. The co-authors can pay the participant amount to attend the conference/workshop(s).
  • If an author has got more than one accepted paper, each paper has to be registered separately.
  • The conference proceedings will be shared in SOFT COPY to the Registered Author ONLY.
  • The registration doesnot cover accommodation, transportation, etc.
  • If you have any queries or clarifications on registration, please email to icacec2026crc@gmail.com
